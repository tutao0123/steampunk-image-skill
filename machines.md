# Subject → machine

Always pick a machine that could exist in a 19th-century shop drawing. The topic is the *job of the machine*, not a scene around it.

| Topic | Hero machine | Required logic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cycling / city ride | Brass safety bicycle with chain drive | Chain + chainring + sprocket meshing |
| Music / concert / tea house | Pipe organ, phonograph, or steam calliope | Pipes or horn + valve / crank |
| Coffee / tea / food | Vertical boiler + pressure group | Boiler + pipe routing + gauge |
| Science / AI / lab | Difference engine or orrery | Gear train with a readable ratio |
| Travel / map / railway | Locomotive cutaway or semaphore | Piston + boiler or lever signal |
| Time / memory / history | Turret clock or chronometer | Escapement + gear train |
| Industry / labor / foundry | Steam hammer or riveter | Piston travel + anvil |
| Ocean / port | Marine steam engine or capstan | Crank + gears |
| Night / moon / sky | Orrery or lighthouse lamp mechanism | Gears + lamp house |
| Writing / press / news | Letterpress platen + ink disk | Piston-like platen + gears |

Anti-mappings (do not do these):

- Topic "cycling" → a person with goggles on a floating penny-farthing
- Topic "AI" → a robot head with steam
- Topic "city" → a skyline with random zeppelins
- Topic "music" → a character playing a saxophone made of pipes

If nothing fits, design a **named apparatus** (`THE {WORD} ENGINE`) whose one job is the topic, and show that job as gears, piston, boiler, pipes, or gauges.
