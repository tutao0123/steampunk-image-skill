# Layouts

All five are 3:4 portrait plates with parchment margins. Pick one.

## hero-assembly (default)

Large mechanical assembly centered. Title stamped in the top margin. Legend box bottom-right or bottom-left. Callouts with leader lines to 4–8 parts. Light dimension lines on one side only.

Use for event posters and most "make me a steampunk poster of X".

## cutaway

The machine is sliced. Interior is readable: boiler tubes, piston in the cylinder, gear train in a housing. Hatch tones for cut surfaces (rust red or walnut), not neon section colors.

Use when the brief is "how it works", engines, organs, locomotives, espresso boilers.

## exploded

Parts float along a central axis, still aligned, still labeled, still one machine. Not a debris cloud. Maintain a ground shadow / centerline so it stays an engineering plate.

Use for kits, bicycles, instruments, "show the parts".

## gauge-board

A riveted instrument panel is the hero: 5–9 gauges, valve wheels, a steam whistle, a small mechanism peeking behind the board. Date, program, or rules live as engraved plates and stencil labels on the board.

Use when the brief is heavy on information (festival schedule, race rules, exhibition).

## dual-ink

Same as hero-assembly, plus **one** rust-red overprint (a route line, a second title word, a stamp). Misregister that ink slightly. Do not add a third color pass.

Use when two ideas must coexist (city + night ride, tea + music).
